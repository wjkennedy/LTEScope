LTEScope radio firmware

Choose the exact board. T-Display S3 provides continuous IQ over native USB; Heltec V3 and CYD provide gapped wideband snapshots over their USB-to-serial bridges. These images do not drive the onboard displays; the waterfall appears in the LTEscope app. LoRa remains an independent radio path.

Experimental radio firmware: ESP-SDR uses undocumented Wi-Fi-radio controls and may permanently alter analog characteristics. Do not return a flashed board to a certified product. The selected firmware base implements reception only.

Onboard SX1262

Heltec WiFi LoRa 32 V3

Receive-only ESP-SDR snapshots over the onboard CP2102. The onboard SX1262 telemetry path is reserved for the next firmware milestone.

Hold PRG, tap RST, release PRG, then install.

External SX1262

LilyGO T-Display S3

Continuous receive-only IQS1 spectrum source over native USB. LoRa requires an external SX1262 and is not enabled in this image yet.

Hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT, then install.

Experimental / unverified

CYD-2432S028

The image boots on the tested classic ESP32 CYD, but its receiver command protocol has not yet responded through the onboard CH340. Flash for development and recovery testing, not as a working target.

Hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT, then install.
